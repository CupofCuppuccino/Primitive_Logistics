PRIMITIVE LOGISTICS - CONTROLS

MOUSE: Hold the left mouse button on a log and drag it. Release to let go.
SPACE: Hold to push the pallet. Release to slow down.
R: Restore the current checkpoint, including the robot, pallet and checkpoint log allowance.
ESC: Pause or resume. The pause menu also offers checkpoint recovery and return to the menu.

DELIVERY TIPS
Start with three logs. A fourth log waits beside the route.
Move logs from behind the pallet to the front to keep the delivery rolling.
Use logs to bridge pits and give the worker a surface to walk across.
Use a log to push the boulder into its pit.
Tip the robot sideways to get under the low ceiling.
Fast movement and impacts can knock the robot off the pallet.

CHECKPOINTS
Passing a checkpoint saves progress automatically.
R recovers logs that were left behind or fell off-screen.
Early checkpoints restore three logs; checkpoint 3 onward restores four.
CONTINUE DELIVERY resumes the saved checkpoint after restarting the game.
