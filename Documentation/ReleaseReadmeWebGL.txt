PRIMITIVE LOGISTICS - BROWSER EDITION

Log: An easy solution for transport or recording

Deliver a fragile robot across a ruined world using the oldest transport
technology you can find: three logs, a wooden pallet, and questionable judgment.
Collect a fourth log, fill pits, move a boulder, and find a way under a low roof.

Play in a desktop browser with a mouse and keyboard. Click the game to focus it.
See CONTROLS.txt and CREDITS.txt for controls and asset credits.
Progress is stored locally in this browser. Clearing site data clears progress.

ITCH.IO UPLOAD
1. Create or edit the project and choose Kind of project: HTML.
2. Upload PrimitiveLogistics_HTML5.zip without extracting it.
3. Enable "This file will be played in the browser" for that ZIP.
4. Set the viewport to 1280 x 720, enable Click to play and Fullscreen button.
5. Leave Mobile friendly and Scrollbars disabled.
6. Upload the Windows ZIP as an optional download and mark its platform Windows.
7. Save as Draft, preview the page, then publish when ready.

LOCAL PREVIEW
Serve the extracted folder with a local HTTP server; do not open index.html
directly from the filesystem. Gzip decompression fallback is included.

Made with Unity 2022.3.62f3 (international edition).
