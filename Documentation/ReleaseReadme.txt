PRIMITIVE LOGISTICS
Ludum Dare 60 - Old Tech
Windows 64-bit

HOW TO RUN
Extract the entire ZIP into a folder, then run PrimitiveLogistics.exe.
Keep PrimitiveLogistics_Data, MonoBleedingEdge and the Unity runtime files beside the executable.
No Unity installation is required. Keyboard and mouse are required.

STARTING A DELIVERY
Choose NEW DELIVERY to start, or CONTINUE DELIVERY to resume your last checkpoint.
Your checkpoint progress is saved on this computer.
