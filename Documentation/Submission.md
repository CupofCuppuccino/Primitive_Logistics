# Primitive Logistics

## Short submission blurb

Deliver tomorrow's technology with yesterday's tools. Move a fragile robot across a ruined world using three logs, a wooden pallet, and a delivery crew with questionable judgment.

## Game description

**Log: An easy solution for transport or recording.**

Your job is simple: deliver one robot. Your equipment is even simpler: a wooden pallet and three logs.

Drag the logs into place and use them as rollers to keep your cargo moving. Find a fourth log along the way, fill pits, climb steep slopes, push a boulder into a hole, and tip the robot sideways to squeeze under a low ceiling. Every bump matters, so try to keep your cargo on the pallet!

Nine checkpoints let you retry each obstacle and recover any logs left behind. Travel from sunrise to sunset through a hand-drawn wasteland, then discover what your boss really values about old technology.

A short physics puzzle game made for **Ludum Dare 60: Old Tech**.

## Controls

- **Mouse:** drag logs.
- **Space:** hold to push; release to slow down.
- **R:** return to the current checkpoint and recover your logs and robot.
- **Esc:** pause or return to the menu.
